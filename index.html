Sri Ganapathi Seva Samithi

Sri Ganapathi Seva Samithi is a community initiative dedicated to Lord Ganapathi and the development of our village temple.

Through the support and contributions of villagers, devotees and well-wishers, we aim to maintain the temple, organize festivals and encourage community participation.

  • 🙏 Temple development
  • 🪔 Regular pooja and celebrations
  • 🤝 Community service
  • 🌸 Ganesh Utsav celebrations
  • ❤️ Support for village activities
